# ClassicControllerInput

Adds a Classic option that restores climbing and sprinting controls from before PEAK 3.1.0.

Before PEAK 3.1.0, you could tap the trigger to grab a wall and stay on it when you released the trigger or tapped it again. In 3.1.0, **Hold** makes you drop when you release the trigger, and **Toggle** makes you drop when you press it again. Either mode can turn a familiar move into a fall. [PEAK 3.1.0 patch notes](https://steamcommunity.com/games/3527290/announcements/detail/1848664643355717)

Mouse and controller climbing also used to work differently by default. With a mouse, you held the climb button. With a controller, you tapped it to grab and stay on the wall. The update made both devices share the same setting, so if you switch devices without changing it, an input you’re used to can make you fall.

| Mode | Climbing | Sprinting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hold** | Hold the climb button to stay on the wall. Release it to drop. | Hold the sprint button while moving forward. Release it to stop sprinting. |
| **Toggle** | Tap to grab the wall. Tap again to drop. | Tap once to start sprinting. On the second press, sprinting stops when you release the button. |
| **Classic** (this mod) | On controller, tap to grab. You can release or tap again and stay on the wall. Press jump to drop. With a mouse, hold the climb button and release it to drop. | On controller, tap while moving forward to sprint, then tap again or stop pushing forward to stop. On keyboard, hold the sprint key. |

## 简体中文

本模组新增 **经典** 选项，可将攀爬和奔跑行为还原到3.1.0以前。

PEAK 3.1.0 更新前，手柄点一下扳机就能抓墙，松开或再按一下都能继续爬。更新后，选**按住**会在松开扳机时脱手，选**切换**则会在再按一次时脱手。两种模式下，照着原来的习惯操作都可能掉下去。[PEAK 3.1.0 更新公告](https://steamcommunity.com/games/3527290/announcements/detail/1848664643355717)

除此之外，以前鼠标和手柄本身默认的行为就是不同的，鼠标是按住攀爬，手柄是按下切换，这次更新统一了这个行为，导致在二者间切换时，如果没有同步修改设置，很可能会因为操作习惯导致坠落。

| 选项 | 攀爬 | 冲刺 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **按住** | 按住攀爬键才会挂在墙上，松手就掉。 | 一边往前推摇杆，一边按住冲刺键。松开按键就停。 |
| **切换** | 按一下抓墙，再按一下脱手。 | 按一下开始冲刺。第二次按下后，要等松开按键才停。 |
| **经典**（本模组） | 手柄点一下抓墙，松手或再按都能继续爬，按跳跃键脱手。鼠标按住攀爬键继续爬，松手就脱手。 | 手柄往前推时按一下开始冲刺，再按一下或停止前推就会停。键盘要按住冲刺键。 |
